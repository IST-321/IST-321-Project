Viking Hub sound files

Put sound files in this folder with these names (mp3, wav, ogg or m4a all work):

  horn.mp3      Plays each time the alerts horn shakes. Keep it short (under a second);
                Viking Hub fades it out when the shake ends.
  war-cry.mp3   Plays once when a student finishes orientation.

Restart isn't needed: refresh the page after adding a file. With no file here, the horn
shakes silently.

Use sounds you have the rights to, such as Pixabay sound effects (free under the Pixabay
Content License) or Freesound clips marked CC0.
